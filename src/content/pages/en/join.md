WAYU is a union of organisations. It grows not through individual applications but through the youth organisations that decide, collectively, to take their place in the anti-imperialist front.

## How to apply

1. **Write to us.** Send an email introducing your organisation: its name, country, founding date, areas of work, and how to reach a representative.
2. **We talk.** We arrange a meeting to get to know each other's work and to discuss what common action would look like.
3. **The union decides.** Applications are evaluated by the Executive Committee and confirmed at the next congress.

## Send us your writing

Our pages are open to anti-imperialist youth everywhere. If you want to write for WAYU, send the text together with a two-sentence biography and, if you have one, a photograph. Texts may be sent in any language — we will handle the translation.
