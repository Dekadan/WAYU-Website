As an aftermath of the terrorist attack at a Railway Station in St. Petersburg, ten Russian citizens passed away and fourty seven people wounded.

We send our sincere condolonces and solidarity feelings to Russian nation who are courageously resisting against US-backed terrorism as well as other Eurasian countries.

They will not achieve their goals with these bloody actions. The unity of Eurasian nations will defeat their imperialist plans.

Executive Committee of WAYU
