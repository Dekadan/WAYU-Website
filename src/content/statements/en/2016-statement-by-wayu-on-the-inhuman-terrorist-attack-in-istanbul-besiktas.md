In two separate blasts carried out by terrorists yesterday, in the center of İstanbul, Beşiktaş, 38 people were killed including 30 police officiers and over 150 people injured.

We know the real target of this inhuman attack. This brutal terrorist attack hasn’t only been aganist Turkish people. This attack also targeted the unity of Eurasian countries. This attack took place when Turkey began to detached from the Atlantic system and get close to Asia. We know the real responsible of this attack is USA who is the biggest terrorist country of the World. The other terrorist organisations as ISIS, PKK, PYD, TAK are its own puppets. And they target the unity of opressed nations.

We say with all our determination, the plans they try to execute through the terrorist organizations will fail. The nations coping with the imperialism will unite and repulse these vicious plans by clasping around their country.

We convey our condolences to the Turkish nation and express our feelings of solidarity with the families and relatives of the ones who suffer from that attack.

We as the anti-imperialist youth of the World, will continue to struggle pertinaciously aganist imperialism!

**Executive Committee of WAYU**

**11.12.2016**
