A statement usually opens with the event it responds to, states the position of
the union in one paragraph, and closes with a call to the member organisations.

## What the fields do

- **Issued by** appears above the title, next to the date. Use it for the body
  that took the decision: the Executive Committee, a congress, a working group.
- **Signatories** are listed at the end of the statement, under a red star.
  Add one line per organisation.
- **Tags** group statements by subject and drive the filter buttons on the
  statements page.
- **Featured** pins the statement to the top of the homepage.

## Writing statements

Keep the first paragraph self-contained: it is what appears in listings, in
search results and when the statement is shared on social media. If you leave
the description field empty, nothing is shown there.
