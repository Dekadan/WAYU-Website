The attacks the U.S. has relaunched against Iran in an attempt to salvage its shattered reputation will serve no purpose other than to deepen the U.S.'s defeat.

The U.S.'s bloody plans will be defeated by the struggle of all resistance fighters—led by Iran—who are fighting for humanity. Furthermore, the most decisive and devastating blow to U.S. imperialism will come from an alliance of countries—from inner Europe to East Asia—that share the same interests and oppose the unipolar world order.

The blow that Iran has dealt to American imperialism in the Strait of Hormuz has made the world's nations aware of their own power. From now on, American imperialism and its lackeys will no longer be able to act as they please in other straits either.

Those who stand on the side of Iran's resistance are the representatives of the emerging, sharing-based new world, as passengers on the ship of humanity;

Those who side with American imperialism, however, are doomed to be swept away into the salty waters of the sea and the dusty pages of history as passengers on a sinking and rotting ship!

The victory of Iran and all those who resist is a victory that will put an end to imperialist wars.

There is room in the Strait of Hormuz for the corpse of imperialism, but there is no room for imperialism itself!

**WAYU Executive Committee**
