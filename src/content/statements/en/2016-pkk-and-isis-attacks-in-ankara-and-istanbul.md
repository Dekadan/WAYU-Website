On the 13th of March, in the capital of Turkey, Ankara, Kızılay Square, 75 people injured and 37 killed because of a PKK’s terrorist attack. All of the dead and wounded were civilians.

On the 19th of March, in the most famous street of Turkey, İstiklal Street, 5 people were killed and 39 wounded because of the ISIS suicide bomber targeting a tourist cortege.

While the treacher plans of America and Israel fail, when they get closer to the failure, they enable their pawn terrorist organizations. They couldn’t divide Iraq, Iran and Syria. Now they’re playing the Turkey card.

But the plans they try to execute through the terrorist organizations will fail. The nations coping with the imperialism will unite and repulse these vicious plans by clasping around their country.

We, as the World Anti-Imperialst Youth Union, condemn these terrorist attacks and send our condolescens to the Turkish nation and to the relatives of the dead.

We, as the youth oppressed nations, won’t surrender to the bloody plans of America – Israel and their pawn terrorist organizations. Our international anti-imperialst struggle will continue ascendantly.

**Executive Committee of WAYU**
