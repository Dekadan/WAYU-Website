Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, the living proof that the struggle in the lands of Western Asia will never cease, has fallen as a martyr, exactly as he desired. He wanted to depart this world not from the quiet of old age, but while fiercely battling against Zionism and imperialism.

His struggle and his courage are now intertwined with the destiny of all the oppressed nations across the globe.

He has been martyred for us all. Like every revolutionary who refuses to bow down to this rotten and murderous system, he has not died, and he never will.

Iran stands as one of the ultimate vanguard fortresses of resistance against imperialism. This heroic nation cannot be shattered by martyrdom. From Cuba to Iran, from Turkey to Lebanon, from Russia to Iraq, from Africa to Central America, and onward to East Asia...

The independence of resisting nations is being forged, thread by thread, by the likes of Ali Khamenei, Hassan Nasrallah, Qasem Soleimani, and countless other heroes who dedicated their very lives to the fight against imperialism in all our struggles for liberation.

Therefore, we honor the profound struggle and martyrdom of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei with the utmost respect. Our fight against imperialism will march on!
