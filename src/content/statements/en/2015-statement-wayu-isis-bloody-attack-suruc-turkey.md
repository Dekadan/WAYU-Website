We, as the members of World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union, condemn the murderous attack which caused the death of 32 people in Suruc, Turkey. 104 people were severely wounded and almost all of them were students who came to Suruc from all over Turkey.

The AKP government is the main responsible body for that bloddy terrorist attack of ISIS, due to its political attitudes towards the unity of Syria. We should not forget that the attack is the result of imperial motives of US in the Western Asia. Imperialism tries to obtain its own gains by targetting the nations’ unity, arming the terrorist organizations and also supporting its puppet governments on the region. Imperialists’ dreadful plans are the biggest enemies of the humanity.

We convey our condolences to the Turkish nation and express our feelings of solidarity with the families and relatives of the ones who suffer from that attack.

**Executive Committee of WAYU**
