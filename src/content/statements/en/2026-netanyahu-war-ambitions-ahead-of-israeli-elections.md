WAYU strongly condemns Benjamin Netanyahu's ambitions to escalate tensions and spread atrocity in Gaza, the West Bank, and Lebanon, warning that this aggression is a desperate attempt to maintain power and gain electoral advantage at the expense of innocent lives. The union asserts that the entity's political system relies on violence, occupation, and hate speech, where leaders bolster their popularity through brutality. It shows the hypocrisy of those who boast about practising democracy.

Condemning these military actions as crimes against humanity, WAYU calls upon global youth and free forces to act immediately to expose these criminal schemes, stand in solidarity with the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples, and pressure international courts to prosecute Netanyahu and his military commanders as war criminals. Ultimately, the union stresses the moral responsibility of the global community to intervene and demand international justice, affirming that global youth will not allow political crises to be exported at the cost of innocent blood, and that resistance remains the path to dismantling the occupation.

As stated in the famous quote, 'Who remains silent in the face of oppression is a friend of the devil'. The countries of the region must not remain silent in the face of the oppression taking place before their very eyes and must unite against the two great devils, the US and Israel. The only solution lies in the countries of West Asia uniting against imperialism and Zionism.
