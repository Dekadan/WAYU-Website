The recent dreadful crimes of Zionist settlers have shown us the real nature of racism again. A Palestinian family -including an 18-months-old infant- was slaughtered. That unspeakable incident proves again that both the army of occupation and the Zionist settlers have the same criminal record.

They use the barbarian attitude towards the Palestinian people that is just same as the terrorists of ISIS’ attitude.

We, as the members of WAYU, condemn that savage, anti-humanitarian action and we support the Palestinian people in their struggle against Zionism and imperialism.

Hands off Palestine!

**Executive Committee of WAYU**
