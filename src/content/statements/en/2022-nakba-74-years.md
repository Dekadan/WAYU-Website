Israel's occupation of Palestine, one of the greatest tragedies and human rights violations in the world, started 74 years ago today. For 74 years since Nakba Day, the systematic atrocities and terrorist practices of the Zionist Israeli regime against the Palestinian people have continued. Since then, nearly 1 million Palestinian people have had to leave their homeland and tens of thousands of them have been killed by the state of Israel.

However, just as the US imperialism, the biggest supporter of Zionism, is being defeated on all continents, Israel will be upside down in the face of the united resistance of the heroic Palestinians and the West Asian alliance to be established.

Nakba, the Great Disaster, will no longer be for Palestine and the nations of the region, but for the imperialists and Zionists.

We will raise the cause of humanity for Palestine, which is independent, free, and whose capital is Jerusalem!

**World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union**
