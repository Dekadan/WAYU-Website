**To the highest attention of the patriotic, revolutionary, and internationalist youth of the world,**

**We salute in solidarity the thousands of students and academics** organizing democratic protests at dozens of universities in the USA and Europe to give voice to the human cause and the Palestinian people who are being genocided by the Zionist regime!

The USA, which markets freedom and democracy to the world, is now offering violence and handcuffs to innocent university students and academics demanding a ceasefire and freedom for Palestine. **We strongly condemn the fascist consensus of the US and European governments!**

The protests in the West have shown that the youth of the world understand that the Palestinian cause is not just about Palestine. Because the oppression of the imperialist US and Zionist Israeli governments has long gone beyond the borders of oppressed nations.

Anti-imperialist youth has historically been the global brand of uprising against oppression. We believe that this movement of conscience and common sense, which is now growing in the belly of the great evil, will have the needed social and political impact if it is supported with proper global solidarity. For this reason, **we call the anti- imperialist youth of the world to a higher solidarity with Palestine!**

**General Secretariat of WAYU**
