We strongly condemn the military operations that have led by Saudi Arabia with the participation of ten other countries against the Houthi Ansarullah Movement in Yemen.

We salute the resistance of Ansarullah Movement which is one of the most powerful anti-imperialist forces in the region. Today, tens of thousands of Yemeni people are in the streets of the capital to condemn the military operations. We are standing with the Yemeni people who have poured into the streets to raise their voice against the imperialist front which is supported by US aggression.

We demand an immediate stop to the military operations in Yemen.

**World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union**
