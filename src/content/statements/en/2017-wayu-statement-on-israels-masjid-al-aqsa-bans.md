Two days ago, a heartbreaking event took place at Masjid Al-Aqsa located in the Jedusalem city which has been under Israel occupation. After Israeli soldiers told that the enterance of people under 50 to the mosque is banned, Palestinians rightfully and strongly resisted that. Israeli soldiers caused many deaths and wounds in trying to contain that indignation.

It is known by whole world that the zionist regime has been stealing lifes and freedom of Palestinian people. As a trojan country of U.S. in the region, it feels courageous with this support to continue doing its unspeakable deeds. Though it is already seen before many times that the zionists have no hesitation to attack people’s sacreds, this time they dared to aim one of the most important sacred places of whole Muslims, Masjid Al-Aqsa mosque.

This is a malicious attack to Palestinian people’s and the entirety of a belief community’s freedom of worship. We, as WAYU, strongly condemn this action.

The blockades and attacks of zionism result in nothing but sharpening the determination of the oppressed people. There is no American or Israeli bullet to defeat the Palestinian people who have been resisting against oppression for generations and the nations who are determined to take imperalism and its collaborator zionism out of their lands.

Zionism cannot and will not break Palestinian people’s will to live free. Imperialism will certainly be defeated. Israil must hand off from Masjid Al-Aqsa immediately.

Executive Committee of WAYU
