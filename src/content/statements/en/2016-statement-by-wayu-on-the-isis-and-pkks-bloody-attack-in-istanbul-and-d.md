ISIS attacked Sultanahmet yesterday, PKK attacked Diyarbakır today…

Imperialism focused divisive and obscurant terror shows its face in different ways and places. It attacks tourists in Sultanahmet. Burns children in Diyarbakır. We also see the different faces of terror in Syria, Iraq, Yemen, Palestine. In fact, when they take their masks off, only one face appears, it is American imperialism. These different masks belong to their colloborators; zionist Israel, puppet terrorist organizations, Saudi-Qatar regime that’s feeding terror and Erdogan’s government.

Our biggest mission as world youth to drop their masks and to fight against the face hiding behind the mask, American imperialism as a whole. We can only nullificate terror by uniting against our common enemy. It is not enough to say we want peace anymore. We have to defend unity and integrity of the nations against the enemy that’s trying to harm the colloboration of the oppressed nations.

We, as WAYU, send our condolences to our brothers and sisters who died at terrorist attacks all over the world. We invite all the anti imperialist youngs to unite against these attacks.

Executive Committee of WAYU
