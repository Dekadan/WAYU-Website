We express our deepest condolences for Hassan Nasrallah, the General Secretary of Lebanese Hezbollah, and strongly condemn the genocidal Israeli regime's war crimes.

The Zionist Israeli regime, which has repeatedly violated international law and the laws of war with the military, economic, political and diplomatic support of the United States of America, is intensifying its war crimes beyond Palestine towards Lebanon.

We call on the United Nations Security Council, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, the Arab League and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation to take real initiatives to prevent a devastating regional war and to achieve a ceasefire in Palestine.

We point out that the pro-active cooperation of the countries of the region is decisive and we are in favour of Iran, Türkiye, Russia and China responding positively to the calls of the Palestinian resistance for guarantees. Otherwise, we point out that imperialist-Zionist war crimes and aggression will spread to the whole region and ignite a global war.

**General Secretariat**
