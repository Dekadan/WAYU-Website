As the day that the US transfer its Israeli embassy to Jerusalem in May comes closer, the aggression of the Israeli Apartheid occupations increases. Yesterday, in the anniversary of the Land Day dedicated to the commemoration of Palestinian martyrs in 1976, in front of the eyes of all humanity, Israeli bullets and bombs murdered at least 15 Palestinians, three of which were children, and wounded thousands of them.

We, the anti-imperialist youth of the world, once again announce: Your cruelty will not silence our resilience and cannot stop the righteous cause of the Palestinian people. Jerusalem belongs to the Palestinians. We will continue our struggle until the conscience of whole world rise to stop this barbarous occupation and massacres. Palestinian people are not alone. They are righteous and they will certainly win this fight and will return to their occupied houses.

Executive Committee of WAYU
