We express our deepest condolences to the Iranian state and nation for the loss of life of the Honorable Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi and the accompanying Iranian state officials in the tragic helicopter crash.

The nations of the world will never forget the strategic role of the late President Ebrahim Raisi and his comrades in ensuring the Islamic Republic of Iran's supreme commitment to the Palestinian cause and strengthening the friendly relations between the countries of the region and the world.

We pay tribute to the memory of the selfless servants of the new polycentric international order.

**General Secretariat**
