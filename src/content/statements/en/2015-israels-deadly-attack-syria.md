We strongly condemn the recent terrorist attack of Israel on the Golan Heights in Syria and killing a number of Hezbullah fighters and an Iranian General in Quneitra region. We also condemn all terrorist acts of the Zionist regime of Israel.

That attack once again showed us that the Zionist regime’s Israel is on the same line as the Takfiri groups and in coordination with the Pentagon. It was not conformed with any human and moral principles or any international regulations.

Israel and USA eventually began to lose their power on the region. Because of this reason they organised an attack against the powers in the Middle East. The Imperialists are trying to protect their benefits but they won’t succeed. We definitely know that our comrades and our martyrs won’t allow them again.

We would like to express our sincere condolences on the death of our comrades.

**Executive Committee of WAYU**
