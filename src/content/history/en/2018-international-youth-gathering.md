On March 17–23, 2018, WAYU hosted a landmark international youth forum in Istanbul, uniting over 200 young delegates from 22 countries under the slogan “The World’s Youth Will Shape Its Own Future.” The event emphasized anti-imperialist solidarity, national independence, and the defense of public health and education in the face of global exploitation. Participants engaged in panel discussions, workshops, and cultural programs, collectively building bridges of friendship and resistance among youth from different continents.

The gathering was rooted in the philosophy that the youth must be the leading force in resisting imperialist aggression and shaping a multipolar world based on sovereignty, equality, and peace. Delegates from countries such as China, Cuba, Russia, India, Venezuela, Vietnam, South Africa, and Palestine shared their experiences and visions for a just future. The event marked a significant step in forming a common front among patriotic youth movements worldwide and strengthening the ties that will give rise to a new international youth alliance.
