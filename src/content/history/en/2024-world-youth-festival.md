In the festival, which was attended by young people from nearly 200 countries studying and working in all fields, we had the opportunity to convey WAYU’s policies to the youth of the world and to talk about the achievements and crises of the world with young legislators from every continent.

Organizing such a comprehensive event for the Russian Federation, which has become the most sanctioned country in the world, and the prominence of BRICS and regional countries in the event was actually an indication that the actors of a multi-centered world do not succumb to the attacks of the West and that the world continues to change despite a collective attack.
