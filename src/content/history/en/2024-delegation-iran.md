At the invitation of Iran’s Basij youth organization, our delegation visited Tehran to make high-level contacts. As the WAYU and TGB delegation, led by TGB Chairman Kayahan Çetin, we held important meetings with Iranian academics, journalists, youth organizations, and media institutions. A comprehensive meeting was also held with Alaeddin Boroujerdi, Deputy to Ali Akbar Velayati, Special Representative of Iran’s Supreme Leader. The delegation also visited the grave of Martyr Qasem Soleimani in Kerman.

In particular, steps that can be taken against the disinformation of the Western media about Türkiye, Iran, and other Asian countries were discussed.

Our delegation also exchanged views on possible future joint projects of the Turkish and Iranian youth and the youth of the whole world. The observations of our delegation in Tehran and Kerman also revealed that many of the anti-Iranian news reports are false.
