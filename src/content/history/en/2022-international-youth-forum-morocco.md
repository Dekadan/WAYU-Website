The World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU), represented by the Youth Union of Türkiye (TGB), actively participated in the International Youth Forum held in Tangier, Morocco. The event convened youth leaders from 40 countries in a unified front against imperialist aggression, under the theme “War Against Imperialism, Peace For All.” Former TGB Chairwoman Dilek Çınar delivered a powerful keynote, stating: “We are the ones who will answer imperialist-led wars with revolution—either revolution prevents war or war leads to revolution,” emphasizing solidarity with struggles from Palestine to broader anti-imperialist causes.

TGB and WAYU delegates passionately affirmed the legitimacy of anti-imperialist resistance, particularly highlighting Palestine’s struggle against Zionism and U.S. influence in the Middle East. Dilek Çınar underscored that “Palestine’s cause is just and victory is in sight,” calling on the global youth movement to collectively build a multipolar world and prepare for just wars countering imperialism. The forum concluded with a closing declaration pledging continued international youth cooperation for peace and revolutionary justice.
