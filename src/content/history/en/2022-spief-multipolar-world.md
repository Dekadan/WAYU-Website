In June 2022, the World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU), represented by its Turkish Secretariat, the Youth Union of Türkiye (TGB), took part in the 25th St. Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF). As part of the “Friends for Leadership” and “New Generation” programs, over 100 young leaders from 42 countries gathered to discuss the role of youth in building a just and multipolar world. Participants from Russia, India, Nigeria, Serbia, Brazil, and others emphasized cooperation against unipolar hegemony and the promotion of sovereignty, cultural diversity, and economic independence.

WAYU’s delegation highlighted the importance of youth-led solutions to global challenges like sanctions, digital censorship, and food insecurity. Through deep international dialogue, the forum affirmed youth’s growing role in shaping an alternative world order based on equality, justice, and mutual respect. SPIEF 2022 marked a significant step in WAYU’s mission to unite global youth around anti-imperialist values.
