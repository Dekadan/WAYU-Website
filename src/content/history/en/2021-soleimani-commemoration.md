In January 2021, the World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU) organized an online international commemoration marking the first anniversary of the assassination of Iranian General Qasem Soleimani. The event brought together youth leaders and anti-imperialist organizations from Iran, Palestine, Lebanon, Syria, and Türkiye. It served as a platform for reaffirming solidarity among the resistance front in West Asia and for condemning the U.S. military presence in the region.

Participants emphasized General Soleimani’s legacy as a symbol of resistance against imperialism and Zionism. The conference highlighted the shared struggles of the youth across nations that are targeted by global hegemonic powers, and called for increased unity, joint mobilizations, and ideological clarity. WAYU reaffirmed its commitment to building a unified anti-imperialist youth front capable of confronting occupation, sanctions, and foreign intervention through international cooperation and political education.
