On July 25, 2021, the World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU) brought together 11 youth organizations from six countries, Palestine, Türkiye, Iran, Lebanon, Syria, and Yemen, for the International Youth Conference on Independent Palestine. Delegates included youth leaders from Palestinian resistance movements Fatah, Hamas, and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine, along with representatives from Iran’s Basij mobilization force, Lebanon’s Hezbollah Youth and Arab National Youth, Syria’s Revolutionary Youth Union, and Yemen’s Ansarullah. This unprecedented gathering united diverse resistance groups in a common front, transcending longstanding political divides to present a unified stand for Palestinian liberation.

The final declaration, “Against the Imperialist-Zionist Threat, For the Independent State of Palestine, We Are One Front and One Heart,” emphasized solidarity, calling on youth organizations to coordinate regular anti-occupation actions. Signatories pledged to escalate protests and mobilizations against U.S. and Israeli aggression.
