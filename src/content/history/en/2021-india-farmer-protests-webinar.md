On January 17, 2021, the World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU) organized a major international webinar titled “The Greatest Agrarian Uprising in History: The Farmer Protests in India.” The event featured prominent speakers such as Prof. Atul Sood from Jawaharlal Nehru University and activist-student Navkiran Natt. They provided critical insights into the political economy of India’s controversial agricultural reforms and the historic mass mobilization of farmers demanding their repeal.

The webinar was attended by young activists, researchers, and political organizers from across the globe. Participants discussed the broader implications of neoliberal agrarian policies and emphasized the importance of global solidarity with the Indian farmers’ movement. This event reflected WAYU’s ongoing commitment to amplifying grassroots struggles against imperialism and corporate exploitation, especially in the Global South.
