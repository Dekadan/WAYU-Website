From January 23 to 26, 2024, a youth delegation from the World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union (WAYU), represented by the Youth Union of Türkiye (TGB), was in Minsk, Belarus, at the invitation of the Belarusian Republican Youth Union (BRSM). WAYU delegates engaged in high-level dialogue with Belarusian counterparts, including BRSM’s Central Committee officials, and signed a joint memorandum committing to educational, cultural, commercial, and humanitarian cooperation.

The meeting was hosted at Belarus State University. It was officially recognized by Belarus’s Foreign Minister, who praised the forum’s contribution to bilateral youth relations and referenced Mustafa Kemal Atatürk’s emphasis that “thinking about the past is not enough, we must also think about today.” The declaration underlined shared Eurasian solidarity and opposition to Western imperialist interference. WAYU and BRSM agreed to confront Western disinformation and geopolitical pressure in education, media, and culture, and pledged to promote the values of sovereignty and peace, echoing Atatürk’s principle “Peace at home, peace in the world.” Belarusian leaders affirmed their commitment to defend national sovereignty and avoid Western-driven “color revolutions.”
