We did not allow imperialism’s plans to rein in the world through terror and aggression. We announced to the whole world that the youth will never surrender to terror!

Against imperialist aggression, terror, and futurelessness, young delegates from across the continents gathered in Türkiye for the International Anti-Imperialist Youth Meeting.
