**This is example content. Delete it from the admin panel before the site goes live.**

Articles are the opinion and analysis section of the site. Unlike statements,
they carry a byline: the **Author** field points at an entry in the Authors
collection, which is what puts the name under the headline and collects the
piece on that author's own page.

## Linking an author

Pick the author from the dropdown in the admin panel. If a contributor writes
in both English and Turkish, create their profile in both languages using the
same file name — the site falls back to the English profile when a translation
is missing, so a byline never breaks.

## Republishing a text

When a piece first appeared somewhere else, fill in **Original source** and
**Original source URL**. A credit line then appears at the end of the article,
linking back to where it was first published.

## Images

The **Image** field is the picture at the top of the article and the one used
in listings and social previews. Always fill in **Image alt** as well: it is
what a reader using a screen reader hears in place of the picture.
