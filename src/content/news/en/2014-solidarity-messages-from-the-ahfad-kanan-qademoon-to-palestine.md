Solidarity Messages from the Ahfad Kana’n (Qademoon) to Palestine:

“Today is the 29th of November. It’s the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People. We stand here against imperialism and Zionism.

Today the World Anti-imperialist Youth Union has different events in different countries to support Palestine and also here in Jordan, we are participating in this event where people came to say they are supporting Palestinians in their struggle against imperialist Zionism and also to say no to Palestine Partition Resolution No. 181 declared by UN in 1947, because all Palestinian people reject this resolution and insist that Palestine is one unity.”
