Hundreds of thousands of people in Baghdad, the capital of Iraq, marched against the corruption and sectarianism within the Iraqi regime. People shouted **“Secularism, secularism, no to Sunni, no to Shia!”** against the ethnic discrimination which was carried by the US occupation forces.

Protestors also called for the Energy Ministers to resign due to the power cuts. They chanted **“Friday after Friday, we’ll get the corrupt out!”**

**Iraq Communist Party** which is a member of WAYU has also attended the protests. We, as the members of World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union, support the struggle of the oppressed people all around the world against imperialism for independence, freedom and democracy!
