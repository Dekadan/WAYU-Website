Solidarity messages from the Communist Party of Southern Switzerland to Palestine;

“The continuous Israeli aggression against Palestine is linked to the global offensive of imperialism in the Middle East, in the context of the west capitalist crisis. The goal is to subjugate the peoples, Balkanise states and steal natural resources. After a rally and a public conference in the last months, the Communist Party of Southern Switzerland expresses also today, November 29th International Solidarity Day for Palestine, its support to the Palestinian national liberation struggle against the racist and terrorist policy of the zionist regime. We ask to the Federal Council of Switzerland to call the Israeli ambassador in Bern and to proceed with diplomatic and economic pressure against Tel Aviv.”
