On April 19, the elections took place in **The Republic and Canton of Ticino** (a canton of the Swiss Confederation in the south of the country) to renew the parliament which is composed of 90 MPs.

**The Communist Party** built an alliance with another left movement and they received 1,45% of the votes. With that alliance, two MPs get into parliament and one of these is our comrade **Massimiliano Ay**, the General Secretary of the Communist Party and the member of the Executive Committee of WAYU (World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union).

We congratulate our comrade. Together, we’ll shape the future. The Communist Party goes a step forward on the road to the victory.

We’ll struggle until the final victory!

**World Anti-Imperialist Youth Union**
